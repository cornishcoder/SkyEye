## B. Current Pipeline Analysis

1. **Camera Capture (`Pixel 10 Pro`)** — [`src/SkyEye.Camera/CaptureEngine.java`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Camera/CaptureEngine.java#L51-L54):
   - Uses `CameraDevice.TEMPLATE_RECORD` with `CONTROL_AF_MODE_CONTINUOUS_VIDEO` and `CONTROL_AE_TARGET_FPS_RANGE(30, 30)` at `1920×1080` H.264 (`6 Mbps`, `1 s` I-frame interval) over SRT.
   - **Architectural Weakness**: Neither Auto-Exposure (`CONTROL_AE_LOCK`) nor Auto-White-Balance (`CONTROL_AWB_LOCK`) is locked, and `CONTROL_AF_MODE` remains continuous. Adding or removing a bright white cue ball or an arm entering the frame causes the camera ISP to shift image luminance and RGB gains even when room lighting is completely constant.
2. **Frame Decoding & Timestamp Pairing (`Pixel 11 Pro XL`)** — [`src/SkyEye.Companion/PlayerTableFrameSource.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Companion/PlayerTableFrameSource.cs#L15-L407) & [`src/SkyEye.Companion/DecodedFrameLedger.java`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Companion/DecodedFrameLedger.java#L1-L35):
   - Pairs ExoPlayer decoded frames (`PixelCopy` / `TextureView`) with `DecodedFrameLedger` (`renderIndex`, `presentationTimeUs`, `realtimeMs`), rejecting stale (`> 750 ms`) or drifted (`> 250 ms`) frames. **Strength**: Clean, well-fenced frame provenance.
3. **Pre-Detection Planar Homography & Pocket Masking** — [`src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs#L259) & [`src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs#L634-L646):
   - Warps the `1920×1080` perspective image into an `800×1600` (or `1000×2000` fallback) 1:2 portrait image via `Cv2.WarpPerspective` using the 4 corner calibration points before detection, and masks pocket corners with `PocketExclusionRadius = 48` at width `1000` (`38.4 px` at width `800`, [`VisionSettings.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionSettings.cs#L24)).
   - **Architectural Weakness**: As shown in [`raw-and-rectified-pocket-coverage.png`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/docs/evidence/pocket-warp-coverage-audit-20261031/raw-and-rectified-pocket-coverage.png), the 4 calibration corners sit on the inner cushion noses and **cut straight through all six pocket jaws**. Warping strictly `[0..W, 0..H]` clips any ball resting in a pocket jaw at the image border (`x=0, x=W, y=0, y=H`), while 3D sphere height ($2R$) projects balls near cushions radially outward by `14–27 px` (`0.65–1.25` ball radii).
4. **Candidate Proposal & Recovery Cascade** — [`VisionAnalyzer.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs#L648-L820), [`PackedBallClusterRecovery.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/PackedBallClusterRecovery.cs#L18-L620), [`DarkBodyRecovery.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/DarkBodyRecovery.cs#L17-L220), [`CueBallRecovery.cs`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/CueBallRecovery.cs#L60-L349):
   - Combines static HSV cloth masking (`H = 82..132, S >= 30, V >= 30`) + `HoughCircles` with specialised recovery passes for packed racks, dark bodies (`V <= 40`), and neutral cue balls.
   - **Architectural Weaknesses**:
     - Under warm/dim/lights-off illumination (`off-01.png`, `250.png`, `266.png`), the blue cloth loses blue dominance (`R >= B`), collapsing HSV saturation and breaking static cloth masks.
     - [`PackedBallClusterRecovery.FindPeakSeeds`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/PackedBallClusterRecovery.cs#L506) has a hard scanline loop cap `for (var i = 1; i < componentCount && i <= 64; i++)`, silently dropping valid black-ball components lower in scanline order whenever upper-table rail shadows produce $>64$ connected components.

## D. Ranked Solutions

### 1. Fix Proposal & Refinement Bottlenecks + Targeted Inventory Resolution (Highest Immediate Impact)
- **What to Change**:
  1. **Targeted Inventory Resolution** ([`VisionAnalyzer.cs:1174-1228`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs#L1174-L1228)): When `yellowIndices.Count > 7` or `redIndices.Count > 7`, never demote all 8 balls to `unknown`. Rank the 8 candidates by chromaticity/cue-whiteness score; if `cueIndices.Count == 0` and the most achromatic/brightest yellow candidate satisfies relaxed cue criteria, reclassify it as `cue`; otherwise demote only the single lowest-confidence surplus candidate.
  2. **Uncapped Component Ranking & Hole-Filled Distance Peaks** ([`PackedBallClusterRecovery.cs:188-520`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/PackedBallClusterRecovery.cs#L188-L520), [`CueBallRecovery.cs:195-290`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/CueBallRecovery.cs#L195-L290)): Remove the `i <= 64` scanline component cap in `FindPeakSeeds` and the `candidates.Count > 4` abort in `RecoverNeutralCueBodies` (filter by area/bounds and sort by score before taking top-$K$). Fill small internal bright holes (the `"8"` spot and specular glint) inside compact dark contours before computing distance transforms.
  3. **Occlusion-Aware Arc Support in `BallBoundaryRefiner`** ([`BallBoundaryRefiner.cs:400-617`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Core/Analysis/BallBoundaryRefiner.cs#L400-L617)): When a `black` or `cue` candidate lies within `1.8 * expectedRadius` of a cushion border, pocket opening, or another detected ball, treat sectors facing the border/pocket/neighbour as *occluded* rather than `RejectExterior`, and evaluate the `0.65` support ratio over the *unoccluded cloth-facing arc* (with a floor of `14/32` valid cloth-facing sectors).
- **Expected Benefits**: Resolves `F1`, `F2`, `F3`, and `F4` simultaneously. Raises `Normal-Validation Layout03` colour accuracy from `43/62 (69.4%)` to `62/62 (100%)`, `Normal-Train Layout03` from `56/62 (90.3%)` to `62/62 (100%)`, and `Transfer New Controls` from `106/109 (97.2%)` to `109/109 (100%)`.
- **Risks & Dependencies**: Pure C# changes in `SkyEye.Core` and `SkyEye.Vision.Android`; must guard against rail/pocket false positives by requiring valid cloth background along the unoccluded interior arc (`>= 14/32` sectors) and verifying `0` false positives on empty-mouth controls (`542, 562, 461, 206, 978`).
- **Offline Validation**: Run [`aggregate_regression_corpus.py`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/docs/argon/scripts/aggregate_regression_corpus.py) and `SkyEye.Checks` across all 32 reviewed frames plus negative pocket-mouth controls.

### 2. Cloth-Anchored Chromaticity Normalisation & Camera2 AE/AWB Lock (Solves Variable Domestic Lighting)
- **What to Change**:
  1. **Every-Frame Cloth Reference Normalisation** ([`VisionAnalyzer.cs:259-410, 648`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs#L259-L410)): Instead of normalizing white balance only when a single cue ball is found (`cueIndices.Length == 1`) and thresholding static HSV (`H = 82..132, S >= 30`), sample the trimmed median and MAD of the interior cloth pixels in CIE Lab / opponent colour space on every frame. Scale channel gains so the median cloth maps to a canonical reference, and segment non-cloth foreground by relative chromatic + luminance Mahalanobis distance from the live cloth distribution.
  2. **Camera2 AE/AWB Lock on Session Start** ([`CaptureEngine.java:51-54`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/src/SkyEye.Camera/CaptureEngine.java#L51-L54)): Allow a brief (~1.0 s) AE/AWB convergence window when live capture starts (or on an explicit `Recalibrate lighting` command), then set `CaptureRequest.CONTROL_AE_LOCK = true`, `CaptureRequest.CONTROL_AWB_LOCK = true`, and fixed focus distance, reporting the locked exposure/gain values in `/status`.
- **Expected Benefits**: Eliminates within-sequence AE/AWB jumps (`2831 vs 542`, `678 vs 455`) and allows the cloth mask and ball classifier to work seamlessly when warm/dim lighting shifts the blue cloth into neutral/warm grey (`off-01.png`, `250.png`, `266.png`).
- **Risks & Dependencies**: The `VisionAnalyzer` cloth normalisation is 100% testable offline on existing images; the `CaptureEngine.java` lock applies only when the camera APK is next updated.
- **Offline Validation**: Validate on [`Sheet 3`](file:///c:/code/SkyEye/docs/argon/contact-sheets/sheet_03_lighting_strata_and_ae_awb_drift.png) lighting holdouts (`daylight`, `all-lights`, `mid-light`, `lights-off`, `warm-266`, and AE/AWB drift pairs).

### 3. Padded Homography Canvas, 3D Sphere Parallax Correction & ROI-Only High-Res Fallback (Solves Geometry & `SampleGap`)
- **What to Change**:
