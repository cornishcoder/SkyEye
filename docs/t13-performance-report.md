2. **Architecture:** immediate reuse of one hardware AVC encode for bounded LAN phone streaming; requested decoded-frame vision; explicit shared commands; independent typed surface state and annotations; stored lightweight phone transform; requested connected-TV composition.
3. **Before/after latency:** user approximately ten seconds → less than one second; final texture-clock estimate 793/986 ms. No claimed physical <500 ms.

5. **Still expensive:** CPU texture readback and OpenCV when requested; optional corrected/annotated TV composition and re-encode; explicit recorded geometry/render/export. A complete receive/decode/display split and companion thermal profile remain unmeasured.
6. **Transport:** keep HLS for archive/replay/direct Chromecast and the measured immediate TS path for the phone. Do not broaden transport replacement based on theory. Further phone latency work must isolate decoder buffering/scheduling and physical event/display correlation; preserve camera-monotonic journal mapping.
