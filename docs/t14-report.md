# T14 independent corrected TV and interrupted preparation

Implementation and physical checks, 6 October 2026. The runtime is implemented; the complete T14 endurance, network recovery and quality acceptance gate remains open.

## Delivery ownership

An explicit connected-TV correction or annotation request starts `BackgroundTvService`, an Android foreground media-playback service with a partial wake lock. Its Media3 decoder feeds an external GLES texture; the stored homography, historical scene and watermark are composed into a hardware AVC encoder surface. It owns its decoder, media server and camera-clock annotation journal. Activity visibility, phone playback and `TextureView` do not supply TV frames. Live TV is silent.

Phone correction retains the inexpensive stored-calibration `SceneProjection` / `TextureView.setTransform()` path. Full table perception remains requested-only; no periodic `VisionAnalyzer`, pocket detection or ball analysis is needed to maintain either corrected live view. Plain TV returns to direct camera delivery and releases the compositor. Geometry/source/timeline changes replace the service lease; changed source dimensions invalidate its correction. Stored calibration restoration after the Pixel 11 reboot required no perception.

Video never makes a CPU bitmap round trip. Only a changed annotation/watermark layer creates and uploads an overlay bitmap. Frame notifications are bounded/coalesced, encoder draining is bounded, completed segments are capped at 16 and the playlist at eight. Closing the service removes generated segments; startup reclaims only its UUID-named private cache directories left by process death. Receiver and camera-status loss have a 30-second grace, then release the pipeline. An explicit returning receiver can request a fresh pipeline. Reboot/force-stop does not silently restore a TV request.

TV scene history uses the independent decoder's source clock and generation, with clears, expiry and gaps retained. Reconnect clears the native active scene rather than keeping old advice. This does not extend voice integration or claim new physical annotation alignment evidence.

## Interrupted exports

Permanent preparation retains a checksum-verified original and frozen annotation journal separately from unpublished derivatives. Retry after cancellation/process restart revalidates the original and reuses that historical journal; current suggestions cannot be substituted during recovery. Both orientations and the manifest still publish together. Failed/cancelled partial derivatives stay out of the library. Recovery is capped at two sources and 1 GiB; successful publication releases it. Permanent originals and camera clips are retained. Restart recovery is available on retry, not an automatically resumed job queue.

The latency follow-up requests half-second keyframe/segment intervals with no B frames and retains at least three advertised target durations before publication. Sub-second encoder keyframe intervals are supported by [Android's media format contract](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/MediaFormat#KEY_I_FRAME_INTERVAL). An owned composed live stream gets one [Cast seek](https://developers.google.com/android/reference/com/google/android/gms/cast/MediaSeekOptions.Builder#setPosition(long)) after playback begins, if the receiver reports a seekable live range. It targets one second behind that range's end to leave delivery headroom. It never seeks saved clips or periodically chases the edge. Receiver-reported range lag is a diagnostic, not camera-to-screen latency; the physical measurement remains authoritative.

## Remaining acceptance

The corrected TV foreground/background/lock implementation replaces the former foreground-only renderer. T14 still needs successful automatic network/receiver recovery on the actual devices, sustained unplugged endurance, physical TV latency, room-audio synchronization and moving annotation/clear/expiry correlation through lock and seek. Historical correction coverage remains independently limited by uncertain original corner intervals. Do not describe every recording as universally corrected or mark the complete T14 gate passed from these builds and samples.
