1. **Recover cue-scratch evidence without relaxing whole-table obstruction.** In the code37 run, every cue-absent sample was obstructed and the cue returned before a clear view. Its logged centre starts stable around `(0.444, 0.497)`, then appears at `(1.011, 0.485)`, `(1.918, 0.476)`, `(1.934, 0.889)`, and disappears. This could be motion toward the far corner, but the cue labels are per-frame detector IDs rather than tracks; those observations are all obstruction/layout-uncertain, and the black count also drops during the sequence. Treat this as ambiguous detection output, not a trajectory or confirmed scratch. Retained clip metadata is available. HTTP-client and streamed raw MP4 requests returned 403 with the camera's reconnect/refresh message; a header-only direct probe returned 200, but no full media body/hash was verified. Resume by stabilizing supported clip access or using another retained local source, then inspect cue movement and pocket-boundary evidence. Do not convert obstructed counts into a pot/scratch.
2. Fixed in code38: `LiveDetectLogStore` trims by both byte and record limits back to 75% when the hard cap is reached. Focused checks write 600 large records and 2,001 small records, verify both caps, and confirm the newest record survives reopening. The physical run exercised this path: after new records, the retained log measured 3,906,031 bytes / 1,685 records, within the 4 MiB / 2,000-record hard caps, and included the new run's final `stopped` record. 4. Fixed in source: `LiveDetectCountCandidateTracker` confirms repeated returns/inventory changes and requires repeated packed-rack evidence for a rack event. It does not treat full spread counts alone as a rack. Physical return/re-rack coverage passed in code37.
5. Count changes alone do not identify a pocket or prove a physical pot. Use existing source-time observation/tracking seams and retained before/after evidence; preserve uncertainty where a hand covers a ball. Avoid creating another canonical match store.
