## Corrected claims and retained limits

- **Timing:** [ShellLiveDetect](../../src/SkyEye.Companion.Hybrid/ShellLiveDetect.cs) calls `table.UpdateAsync` and logs `observation.FrameAgeMs`. [TableAnalysisSession.RunUpdateAsync](../../src/SkyEye.Core/Analysis/TableAnalysisSession.cs) assigns that age after `analyzer.Analyze`. It is not merely pre-analysis decode age. Adding Code70's 894ms p95 age to its 491ms p95 pass time double-counts overlapping work; sums of independent percentiles also do not yield an end-to-end p95. Measure same-frame acquisition, analysis and publication endpoints. The [800-to-1000 fallback](../../src/SkyEye.Vision.Android/VisionAnalyzer.cs) exists for detected incomplete packed clusters, but its frequency and total cost on the target frames require measurement.
- **Label precision:** 4–7 source pixels cannot be directly compared with a radius in an 800px rectified image. Verify the local transform, uncertainty and scoring target. Independently reviewed source crops/rims can improve labels; the candidate's own fitted centres cannot supply independent truth. Audit apparent image-centre versus physical cloth-plane centre/parallax. Do not adopt `.50R` matching or `.35R` p95 limits automatically; freeze justified units and limits before evaluation, retaining the existing `.25R` proposal unless evidence supports a documented revision.
- **Hybrid pivot:** accept a decision checkpoint after at most three measured geometry candidates fail matched Development criteria. Use existing [O09](../o09-learned-ball-detection-spike.md) seams for a bounded on-device proposal-plus-C# verifier benchmark. Local-model proposal counts are not reviewed held-out accuracy, and `<40ms` on Pixel is an unmeasured suggestion. Choose runtime and model from measured compatibility, resources and accuracy; do not prescribe TFLite, ONNX or NNAPI without that work.
- **Model preference:** remove the stale checkout anchor, but preserve the user's explicit Luna/max preference for available, permitted subagent audits with a direct-work fallback. Do not claim that a delegated model changes the parent chat's model.
- **Progress discipline:** replace a blanket ban on prose commits with a concrete-result requirement per work cycle. Review decisions, provenance fixes and precise blockers can be necessary evidence; repeated audits without a new question or result are not progress.
